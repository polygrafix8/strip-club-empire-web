What You Actually Do

Think Two Point Hospital energy in a gentlemen’s club. You’re not watching a slideshow — you’re managing a business with teeth.

01

Design the money floor

Place stages, bars, seats, lights, and walls. Pathing, dark corners, and bottlenecks change spend, stress, and what staff can hide.

02

Run the night live

Patrons drink, tip, fight, and need the bathroom. Direct your manager, put out fires, and keep service flowing before satisfaction tanks.

03

Admin the empire

Hire from applications, set schedules, door price, bar markup, and house cut. Cheap hires save cash — until compliance bites.

⭐

Reputation is the score

Stars change who walks in — cheap no-spenders or ballers and VIPs — and how busy the door gets.

🚨

Risk vs reward

Systems With Teeth

Adult theme. Pure tycoon under the hood. Every profit lever can raise risk.

🏗️

Design mode

Stages, bars, décor, room lights, stage lights. Light dark corners or watch rule-breaking hide. Chokepoints slow drinks and create messes.

🍸

Operations

Real-time floor management. Click-to-move manager, radial actions, substitute for missing staff, presence that makes underperformers behave.

📋

Admin office

Applications, weekly schedules, admission price, bar markup, house commission. Vet paperwork or hire a future raid.

⭐

Reputation

Your score and your traffic engine. Better stars → better patrons and more volume. Raids slash reputation hard.

🚨

Heat & compliance

Shady dancers, bad patrons, forged papers, undercover vice. A raid clears the room, fines you, and can end the campaign if you stack them.

💰

Money with tradeoffs

Door fees, dancer house cut, bar markup — plus VIP rooms later. Squeeze too hard and dancers quit or drunks detonate the night.

Hire Carefully

Dancers pay the house a cut. Everyone else is a wage. Applications hide quality and risk — cheap talent can cost more than their salary.

Dancers
